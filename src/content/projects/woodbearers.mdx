The Woodbearers is an Virtual Reality Experience hosted by the [New Mexico Museum of Art - Vladem Contemporary](https://www.nmartmuseum.org/vladem-contemporary/) that is based on [Clark Huling's Woodbearers of Chimayo Painting in 1974](https://www.clarkhulings.com/paintings/). I was the lead developer on the project at the [VAST Lab]({{site.url}}/vast-lab/).
