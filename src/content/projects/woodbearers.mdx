The Woodbearers is a Virtual Reality Museum Experience where people can walk into and look around the painting, created by the [VAST Lab]({{site.url}}/vast-lab/) and hosted by the [New Mexico Museum of Art - Vladem Contemporary](https://www.nmartmuseum.org/vladem-contemporary/).

<VideoEmbed videoId="g29ADLrQ65E" />

The goal of the project was to make an eye-catching, educational, and immersive experience for people to better appreciate and explore through a work of art.

The original paining is the ["Woodbearers of Chimayo", by Clark Hulings in 1974](https://www.clarkhulings.com/paintings/).

<ImageEmbed file="woodbearers/original_painting.jpg" />

The goal of the experience is for people to be able to walk into and around the world of the painting, exploring the minor details.
