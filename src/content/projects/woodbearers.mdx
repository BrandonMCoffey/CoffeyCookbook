The Woodbearers is a Virtual Reality Experience where people can walk into and around a painting, created by the [VAST Lab]({{site.url}}/vast-lab/) and hosted by the [New Mexico Museum of Art - Vladem Contemporary](https://www.nmartmuseum.org/vladem-contemporary/).

<VideoEmbed videoId="g29ADLrQ65E" />


For the project, I was the lead technical artist and programmer

The goal of the experience is for people to be able to walk into and around the world of the painting, exploring the minor details and 
that is based on [Clark Huling's Woodbearers of Chimayo Painting in 1974](https://www.clarkhulings.com/paintings/). I was the lead developer on the project at the .
